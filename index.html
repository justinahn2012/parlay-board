  • Draw a loop around one kind of animal to rope them. The sun is your clock: every animal you rope holds it up a little longer.
  • Bigger loops pay more: 5+ in one loop ×2, 10+ ×2.5, 20+ ×3, 30+ ×4, 40+ ×5, rounded up.
  • Sheep and pigs are worth 1 point, cows 2. Later in the day come horses worth 3 (45 s), longhorns worth 4 (1½ min) and bison worth 5 (2½ min). About once a minute a big drive crosses the range, bigger each time.
  • Trouble: skunks and porcupines ruin a rope (from 15 s and 45 s), coyotes and wolves steal stock (from 30 s and 75 s; rope one that's carrying an animal to get it back), and locoweed sends you loco. Never rope Biscuit.
  • Tumbleweeds carry power-ups that fire on your next throw, together if you catch several: Cowboy Coffee (increased focus: the ranch slows down while you draw that loop), Lucky Horseshoe (no skunk, porcupine or Biscuit can ruin the loop), Golden Lasso (every kind at once), Dinner Bell (a herd comes running to your finger) and Trick Rope (skips on to more herds of that kind).
